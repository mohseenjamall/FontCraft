## 1.3.0
- Switch any font on or off with one click, right on its row or card
- Turn on or off every font you are looking at with one button
- New grid view: browse fonts as cards, and keep the list if you prefer it
- Collections for Arabic, English, variable fonts and families with more than 3 weights
- A Turned on list shows every font that is on, whoever turned it on
- Type Arabic or English in the preview and only fonts that can show it are listed
