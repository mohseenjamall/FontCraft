# Design context: FontCraft website

Source: the creator, across the build conversation (not inferred from code).

- **Audience:** people on Windows who manage many fonts (designers, developers, Arabic-script users) and find FontBase heavy and slow.
- **Job:** understand in one screen that FontCraft is a faster, lighter, free replacement for FontBase, then download it.
- **Personality:** craft and print: calm, exact, a little warm. An engraver's workshop, not a SaaS landing page.
- **Brand assets:** the cobalt engraving of a hand drawing a serif F (the app image); Fraunces display type; one cobalt/periwinkle accent.
- **Honesty:** free, still in development, Windows now and macOS/Linux later; comparison table states what FontCraft still lacks.
- **Constraints:** static Astro page, GSAP motion with reduced-motion fallback, readable without JS.
